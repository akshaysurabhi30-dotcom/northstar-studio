# HydroATLAS / BasinATLAS feature mapping audit

## Scope and decision rule

This audit maps the **11 final model features** documented in `docs/model_contract.md`. A field is only marked as a usable mapping when its meaning, spatial support, and units are compatible with the variable used during training. A superficially similar field is marked as a candidate—not a substitution—when the training source does not establish equivalence.

HydroATLAS has BasinATLAS attributes for sub-basin polygons and uses a consistent field syntax: layer, unit, spatial support, and temporal/statistical dimension. Its documentation defines `s` as “in sub-basin,” `p` as “at pour point,” `u` as total upstream watershed, `av` as average, and `yr` as annual average. [HydroATLAS Technical Documentation, pp. 6–7](https://data.hydrosheds.org/file/technical-documentation/HydroATLAS_TechDoc_v10_1.pdf)

## Blocking issues before live HydroATLAS inference

1. **`area` is ambiguous.** The model’s training materials do not say whether it represents the individual site catchment, total upstream drainage area, or another catchment definition. `SUB_AREA` and `UP_AREA` are different measurements and must not be interchanged.
2. **`altitude` is ambiguous.** BasinATLAS average elevation is not automatically equivalent to an observation/site elevation.
3. **`dis_mean` is ambiguous.** BasinATLAS `dis_m3_pyr` is modeled, natural, long-term, annual-average discharge at the pour point. The training material does not establish that `dis_mean` shares its unit, source, timing, naturalization, or spatial support.
4. **Land-cover categories have no established crosswalk.** HydroATLAS offers land-cover variables, but the supplied training data does not define how its `Broadleaf`, `Mixed`, and `Needleleaf` labels were created. The three model indicator features therefore cannot be constructed from HydroATLAS without a documented mapping.

## Implementation consequence

The app can safely use BasinATLAS for watershed selection and can derive calendar features locally. It must **not** make a live model prediction from HydroATLAS until the four blocking semantic mappings above are resolved, or until the model is retrained with explicitly documented HydroATLAS fields.
